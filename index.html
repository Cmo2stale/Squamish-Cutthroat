Backup

Surveys live only on this phone. Save a backup after each field day and keep it somewhere safe.

How backups work
  • What it is: one file with every survey, fish observation and redd on this phone.
  • Where to keep it: email it to yourself or the project lead, or save it to Files, Google Drive or iCloud.
  • It looks strange, and that's normal. It's the app's own format (text, quotation marks, long ID numbers), not a spreadsheet. Use Export for a readable copy.
  • Don't edit it. Changing anything can stop it from restoring.
  • To restore: tap Restore from a backup and pick the file. Restoring only adds or updates records. It never deletes any.
Your data

Nothing is uploaded. Surveys stay on this phone until a crew member exports or backs them up and chooses who to send them to.

Type
Species
Quick ID: is it a cutthroat?
  • Light spots on a dark body, no black on the dorsal fin: char (bull trout / Dolly Varden).
  • White-then-black leading edge on the anal fin (small fish): coho.
  • Red-orange slash + spots below the lateral line + teeth behind the tongue: CCT.
  • Spots mostly above the lateral line, pink band, no slash or tongue-base teeth: rainbow / steelhead.
  • Mixed or unclear: CCT/RB unresolved and take a photo. Don't guess.

Confidence
Which confidence?
  • Confirmed: classic pit-and-tailspill with cleaned gravel, or a fish seen on it.
  • Probable: clear cleaned patch with the right shape and location, some ambiguity.
  • Possible: suggestive disturbance you can't separate from scour or trampling.
